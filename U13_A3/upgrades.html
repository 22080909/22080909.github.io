Why you should upgrade?

Upgrading allows you to experiance the same brand but with better features, with technology evolving every day you can experiance the best and early too! Also upgrading your SIM contract can help you with either more data or more free calls for overseas, With the UK data plans being limited to 25GB overseas (Depending on Country) Having the right data plan can be advantagous to you! The network provider also matters when choosing a plan as some providers use other networks instead of hosting their own, which can be slow at times.
